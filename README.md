# ⚽ FIFA World Cup Dashboard – Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

The **FIFA World Cup Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project created using **Tableau**. The project analyzes FIFA World Cup data from **1930 to 2014** to understand tournament history, team performance, hosting countries, qualification, stadiums, competition statistics, and audience distribution.

The dashboard transforms historical World Cup data into interactive visualizations that make it easier to identify trends and compare countries and teams across different tournaments.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze FIFA World Cup history from **1930 to 2014**
* Identify countries that have won the World Cup
* Analyze teams reaching the **Finals and Semi-Finals**
* Identify countries that hosted World Cup tournaments
* Analyze top-performing teams by World Cup year
* Explore Finals stadiums
* Analyze countries qualified for each World Cup
* Compare competition statistics across different years
* Visualize World Cup-related data geographically
* Analyze audience distribution by country and year

---

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 🏆 World Cup Winners

Analyzes the countries that won the FIFA World Cup and compares their historical success.

### 🥇 Finals & Semi-Finals

Shows teams based on their total appearances in the **Finals and Semi-Finals**, helping identify consistently successful teams.

### 🌍 World Cup Hosts

Analyzes the countries that hosted the FIFA World Cup over the years.

### ⚽ Top Teams by World Cup Year

Provides a year-wise view of the top-performing teams and their participation in the tournament.

### 🏟️ Finals Stadium Analysis

Analyzes the stadiums where World Cup finals were played and provides insights into the tournament venues.

### 🌎 Qualified Countries

Visualizes the countries that qualified for the FIFA World Cup across different editions.

### 📈 Competition Statistics

Analyzes tournament-level statistics and compares competition trends across World Cup years.

### 🗺️ Geographical Analysis

Uses interactive maps to visualize World Cup-related information by country.

### 👥 Audience Analysis

Analyzes audience distribution by **country and year** to understand the global reach and popularity of the FIFA World Cup.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the `.twbx` Tableau workbook using **Tableau Desktop** or **Tableau Public**.
3. Explore the dashboard using the available filters and visualizations.
4. Select different World Cup years and countries to analyze historical trends.
